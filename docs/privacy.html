Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your family's information.

Daffodils Montessori is a small, family-run school. We collect only what we need to care for children and run the school, and we keep it private.

What we collect

When you contact us, book a tour, or apply, we receive what you type into the form: names, email, phone number, your child's age or date of birth, and any notes you add.

When you create a family account, we store your name, email, and phone number. Inside the portal you may add your child's name, date of birth, allergies, medical notes, emergency contacts, the people allowed to pick up, and documents such as immunization records.

While your child attends, our staff record daily reports, attendance times, lesson progress, conference bookings, and payment records. We may take classroom photos as described below.

How we use it

We use this information to answer your questions, run admissions, care for your child safely, keep required records, communicate with your family, and handle tuition.

We do not sell or rent your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use tracking or advertising cookies on this website.

Who can see it

Your family's records are visible only to you and to Daffodils staff with an administrator account. The database enforces this rule for every request, so one family can never see another family's children, reports, documents, or payments.

Curriculum files and families-only photos are available to currently enrolled families. Download links expire after a few minutes.

Photos

Photos on the public website are chosen by our staff and only include children whose families have signed a photo release. Classroom photos marked "families only" are shown only to enrolled families after they log in. Tell us at any time if you do not want your child photographed or pictured online, and we will honor it.

Services we rely on

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Accounts, records, and files are stored with Supabase, a database provider that encrypts data in transit and at rest. Card payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number, only the amount, date, and status. Copies of website form submissions may be emailed to the school through FormSubmit. The map on our Contact page is provided by Google Maps.

Your choices

You can view and update your family's information in the Family Portal at any time. To request a copy of your records or ask us to delete your account, email us. Some records, such as attendance and immunization records, must be kept for a period of time under Washington child care licensing rules, and we will explain anything we are required to keep.

Children's information

Children do not create accounts or use this website. Information about a child is provided by a parent or guardian, or recorded by our staff while caring for the child.

Cookies and browser storage

When you log in, your browser stores a secure session so you stay signed in. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. Logging out removes the session.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date below and let enrolled families know about important changes. Questions? Email rishiekhanna@yahoo.com or call (425) 623-2684.
