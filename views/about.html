Our Story

A Dream Rooted in Love for Children

Daffodils Montessori was founded in 2010 by Maria Gonzalez, a lifelong educator and Montessori enthusiast who dreamed of creating a space where children could learn through discovery, creativity, and joy.

What began as a small classroom with just 12 students has blossomed into a thriving community of over 100 families. Our campus features purpose-built Montessori classrooms, beautiful outdoor play areas, a children's garden, and a nature trail.

Every detail of our school has been thoughtfully designed to inspire wonder and support the natural development of every child who walks through our doors.

Our Mission

To provide an exceptional Montessori education that nurtures the whole child — intellectually, socially, emotionally, and physically — in a prepared environment that fosters independence, respect, and a love of learning.

Our Vision

To cultivate confident, compassionate, and creative individuals who are prepared to contribute positively to their communities and the world, carrying with them the seeds of curiosity planted during their earliest years.

Our Values

Respect for the child, community partnership, joyful learning, environmental stewardship, diversity and inclusion, and the belief that every child has unlimited potential waiting to be unlocked.

Our Approach

The Montessori Method

Developed by Dr. Maria Montessori over a century ago, this proven educational approach respects each child as a unique individual.

01

Prepared Environment

Our classrooms are carefully designed with child-sized furniture and organized materials that invite exploration and independent work.

02

Self-Directed Learning

Children choose their own activities within the prepared environment, developing concentration, motivation, and self-discipline.

03

Mixed-Age Classrooms

Younger children learn from older peers, while older children reinforce their knowledge through teaching — building a natural community.

04

Hands-On Materials

Beautiful, specially designed Montessori materials engage multiple senses and make abstract concepts concrete and accessible.

05

Uninterrupted Work Periods

Extended work cycles allow children to dive deep into activities, developing focus and the joy of meaningful engagement.

06

Observation-Based Guidance

Teachers carefully observe each child to understand their needs, interests, and readiness, providing individualized support.
