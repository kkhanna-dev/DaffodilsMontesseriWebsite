=========================================================
  STRIPE TUITION PAYMENT SETUP GUIDE
  Daffodils Montessori Website
=========================================================

Hello! This guide will walk you through setting up online
tuition payments on your website. Everything is explained
in plain, everyday language. You can do this!


---------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT IS STRIPE?
---------------------------------------------------------

Stripe is a payment processor -- think of it like a
digital cash register. It lets parents pay tuition with a
credit card or debit card, right on your website.

Stripe handles all the complicated security stuff. Your
website never sees or stores anyone's card number. Stripe
keeps it safe, and just tells your website "payment
went through!" or "payment failed."

Stripe is used by millions of businesses, from tiny shops
to huge companies. It is very safe and very reliable.


---------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW THE TUITION PAGE WORKS
---------------------------------------------------------

Here is what parents see when they visit the Tuition page
on your website:

  a) First, they see the tuition rates displayed for each
     program (Toddler, Primary, Kindergarten, etc.).

  b) Below that, there is a "Make a Tuition Payment"
     section. Parents must be logged in to use it. If they
     are not logged in, they will see a prompt to log in
     or register for an account.

  c) Once logged in, a parent will:
     - Select their child from a dropdown list
     - Pick a payment amount (matching their program) or
       enter a custom amount
     - Add a description (like "March 2026 Tuition")
     - Enter their credit or debit card information
     - Click "Pay Now"

  d) After paying, parents can see their payment history
     on the same page, showing the date, description,
     amount, and whether it succeeded.

If Stripe is not set up yet, parents will see a message
saying "Payment system is not configured" and a phone
number to call instead.


---------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW TO SET UP A STRIPE ACCOUNT
---------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Go to https://stripe.com

Step 2: Click "Start now" or "Create account" (the button
        may look slightly different, but it will be
        obvious on the page).

Step 3: Enter your email, full name, and create a
        password. Then click "Create account."

Step 4: Stripe will ask you to verify your email. Check
        your inbox and click the verification link.

Step 5: Stripe will walk you through setting up your
        business profile. You will need:
        - Your business name (Daffodils Montessori)
        - Your business address
        - Your bank account info (where Stripe will
          deposit the tuition payments)
        - Your tax ID or Social Security Number
          (Stripe is required by law to collect this)

Take your time with this. You only have to do it once.


---------------------------------------------------------
4. GET YOUR API KEYS FROM STRIPE
---------------------------------------------------------

"API keys" are like secret passwords that let your website
talk to your Stripe account. Here is how to find them:

Step 1: Log in to your Stripe account at
        https://dashboard.stripe.com

Step 2: Look at the top-right corner of the page. You
        will see a toggle switch that says "Test mode"
        or "Live mode" (more on this in a moment).

Step 3: In the left sidebar, click "Developers."

Step 4: Click "API keys."

Step 5: You will see two keys:
        - Publishable key -- starts with pk_test_ or
          pk_live_
        - Secret key -- starts with sk_test_ or sk_live_
          (you may need to click "Reveal" to see it)

IMPORTANT: The secret key is like the master key to your
payment system. Never share it with anyone. Never post it
online. Never email it. Only put it in the .env file on
your server (see below).


---------------------------------------------------------
5. TEST MODE vs. LIVE MODE
---------------------------------------------------------

Stripe has two modes. This is really helpful!

TEST MODE (the safe playground):
  - No real money is involved. Nothing gets charged.
  - Your keys will start with pk_test_ and sk_test_
  - Use this to try everything out and make sure payments
    work before going live.

LIVE MODE (the real deal):
  - Real money gets charged to real credit cards.
  - Real money gets deposited into your bank account.
  - Your keys will start with pk_live_ and sk_live_
  - Only switch to this when you are ready for parents
    to make real payments.

You can flip between Test and Live mode using the toggle
at the top of your Stripe Dashboard. Each mode has its
own set of keys.


---------------------------------------------------------
6. HOW TO ADD STRIPE KEYS TO YOUR WEBSITE
---------------------------------------------------------

Your website has a special file called ".env" that stores
private settings. It is in the main folder of your
website files. Here is how to update it:

Step 1: Find the file called ".env" in your website
        folder. It is in the very top level, same place
        as server.js.

        The full path is:
        C:\Users\vlwhi\Repos\DaffodilsMontesseriWebsite\.env

Step 2: Open it with Notepad (right-click the file,
        choose "Open with," then pick Notepad).

Step 3: You will see lines that look like this:

        STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_test_your_key_here
        STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY=pk_test_your_key_here
        STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_your_secret_here

Step 4: Replace the placeholder values with your real
        keys from Stripe. For example, if your keys are:

        Secret key:      sk_test_abc123xyz
        Publishable key: pk_test_def456uvw

        Then change those lines to:

        STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_test_abc123xyz
        STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY=pk_test_def456uvw

        Leave STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET alone for now -- it
        is optional and only needed for advanced webhook
        verification. Your payments will work fine
        without it.

Step 5: Save the file (Ctrl+S).

Step 6: Restart your website server so it picks up the
        new settings. (If you are not sure how to do
        this, ask your web developer or hosting provider.)

IMPORTANT NOTES:
  - Do NOT put spaces before or after the = sign.
  - Do NOT add quotes around the key values.
  - Do NOT share this file with anyone. It contains
    private information.


---------------------------------------------------------
7. HOW TO TEST PAYMENTS
---------------------------------------------------------

Before accepting real money, test everything first!

Step 1: Make sure your .env file has TEST mode keys
        (the ones starting with sk_test_ and pk_test_).

Step 2: Go to your website's Tuition page.

Step 3: Log in with a parent account.

Step 4: Fill out the payment form. When it asks for card
        details, use this special test card number:

        Card number:  4242 4242 4242 4242
        Expiry date:  Any future date (like 12/28)
        CVC:          Any 3 digits (like 123)
        ZIP code:     Any 5 digits (like 12345)

Step 5: Click "Pay Now."

Step 6: You should see a success message! No real money
        was charged -- this is just a test.

Step 7: Go to your Stripe Dashboard and click "Payments"
        in the left sidebar. You should see the test
        payment listed there.

If something goes wrong, double-check that your API keys
in the .env file are correct and that you restarted the
server after making changes.


---------------------------------------------------------
8. SWITCHING FROM TEST MODE TO LIVE MODE
---------------------------------------------------------

When you are satisfied that everything is working with
test payments, here is how to go live:

Step 1: Log in to your Stripe Dashboard.

Step 2: Flip the toggle from "Test mode" to "Live mode."

Step 3: Go to Developers > API keys (just like before).

Step 4: Copy your LIVE publishable key (starts with
        pk_live_) and your LIVE secret key (starts with
        sk_live_).

Step 5: Open your .env file and replace the test keys
        with the live keys:

        STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_live_your_real_key_here
        STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY=pk_live_your_real_key_here

Step 6: Save the file and restart your server.

That is it! Parents can now make real payments. Real
money will flow into your connected bank account
(usually within 2 business days).

If you ever need to go back to test mode (for example,
to try out a change), just swap the live keys back to
test keys in the .env file and restart.


---------------------------------------------------------
9. WHERE TO SEE PAYMENT RECORDS
---------------------------------------------------------

You can view payments in two places:

  A) YOUR WEBSITE'S ADMIN DASHBOARD
     - Log in to your website as an admin.
     - Go to the Admin Dashboard.
     - You will see payment records there, including
       which parent paid, how much, the date, and
       whether the payment succeeded.

  B) THE STRIPE DASHBOARD
     - Log in at https://dashboard.stripe.com
     - Click "Payments" in the left sidebar.
     - Here you will see every payment, with full
       details, refund options, and more.
     - Stripe also lets you export payment data to a
       spreadsheet if you need it for your records.

The Stripe Dashboard is the most detailed view. Use it
if you need to issue a refund, investigate a failed
payment, or pull reports.


---------------------------------------------------------
10. STRIPE FEES
---------------------------------------------------------

Stripe charges a small fee for every payment:

  2.9% of the amount + $0.30 per transaction

For example:
  - A $1,000 tuition payment would cost you:
    ($1,000 x 2.9%) + $0.30 = $29.30 in fees
    You receive: $970.70

  - A $850 tuition payment would cost you:
    ($850 x 2.9%) + $0.30 = $24.95 in fees
    You receive: $825.05

These fees are automatically deducted by Stripe before
the money lands in your bank account. There are no
monthly fees or setup fees -- you only pay when a
payment goes through.


---------------------------------------------------------
11. ABOUT THE TUITION RATES ON THE WEBSITE
---------------------------------------------------------

The tuition amounts displayed on the website (like
"$850/month" for Toddler Half-Day) are just display
text for parents to see. They are not hard-coded into
the payment system.

When a parent makes a payment, they select or type in
the actual amount they want to pay. The displayed rates
are there to show parents what their program costs, but
the payment system is flexible.

TO CHANGE THE DISPLAYED TUITION RATES:
  1. Log in to your website as an admin.
  2. Go to the Tuition page.
  3. You will see a small green pencil button on the
     tuition rate cards.
  4. Click the green pencil button.
  5. Edit the program names, descriptions, or prices
     right on the page.
  6. Save your changes.

That is it! You do not need to touch any files or code
to update the rates parents see.
