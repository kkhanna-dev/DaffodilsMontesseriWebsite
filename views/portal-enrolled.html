Curriculum Areas

What your child is learning this semester across all Montessori areas.

Practical Life

  • Pouring, spooning, and transferring activities
  • Buttoning, zipping, and dressing frames
  • Table setting and food preparation
  • Care of self and environment
  • Grace and courtesy lessons

Sensorial

  • Pink tower and brown stair
  • Color tablets and grading
  • Sound cylinders and bells
  • Geometric solids and shapes
  • Texture and temperature exploration

Language

  • Sandpaper letters and sound games
  • Moveable alphabet work
  • Reading readiness activities
  • Storytelling and oral language
  • Writing practice and journaling

Mathematics

  • Number rods and spindle boxes
  • Golden bead material (place value)
  • Addition and subtraction with materials
  • Counting and number recognition
  • Pattern and sequence work

Cultural Studies

  • Continent and country studies
  • Botany: parts of a plant, leaf shapes
  • Zoology: animal classification
  • Science experiments and observations
  • Art and music appreciation

Creative Arts

  • Painting and drawing
  • Clay and sculpture work
  • Music, rhythm, and movement
  • Dramatic play and storytelling
  • Nature-based art projects
