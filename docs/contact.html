Frequently Asked Questions

We accept children from ages 2 through 6. Our Toddler Program is for ages 2-3, our Primary Program is for ages 3-5, and our Kindergarten Program is for ages 5-6.

Tuition depends on the program and schedule. Our Tuition page always shows the current monthly rates, and we are happy to answer questions.

Yes, we provide freshly prepared, nutritious lunches and snacks daily. Our kitchen accommodates common allergies and dietary restrictions. Parents may also choose to send lunch from home.
