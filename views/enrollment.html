How to Enroll

Enrollment Process

Our simple 5-step enrollment process ensures a smooth start for your family.

1

Schedule a Tour

Visit our campus, observe classrooms, and meet our teachers during a personalized tour.

2

Submit Application

Complete the enrollment application form below with your child's information.

3

Family Interview

Meet with our admissions team to discuss your child's needs and our programs.

4

Acceptance

Receive your acceptance letter and enrollment packet within one week.

5

Welcome!

Complete paperwork, pay enrollment deposit, and join the Daffodils family!
