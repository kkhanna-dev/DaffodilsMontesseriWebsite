Toddler Classroom
Ages 2-3

Toddler Program

Our Toddler Program provides a gentle, nurturing introduction to the Montessori environment. In this safe and stimulating space, young children begin to develop their sense of self and their relationship with the world around them.

Schedule

Half-day: 8:30 AM - 12:00 PM
Full-day: 8:30 AM - 3:00 PM

Class Size

Maximum 12 children with 3 teachers (4:1 ratio)

Tuition

Half-day: $850/month
Full-day: $1,200/month

Curriculum Highlights

  • Practical life activities (pouring, sorting, dressing)
  • Sensory exploration with Montessori materials
  • Language enrichment through songs, stories, and conversation
  • Gross motor development and outdoor play
  • Art and creative expression
  • Social skills and community building
Primary Classroom
Ages 3-5

Primary Program

Our Primary Program is the heart of the Montessori experience. Children work in a beautifully prepared classroom with a full range of Montessori materials, developing academic foundations alongside essential life skills and social-emotional intelligence.

Schedule

Half-day: 8:30 AM - 12:00 PM
Full-day: 8:30 AM - 3:00 PM
Extended: 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Class Size

Maximum 24 children with 3 teachers (8:1 ratio)

Tuition

Half-day: $950/month
Full-day: $1,400/month
Extended: $1,650/month

Curriculum Areas

  • Practical Life: care of self, care of environment, grace & courtesy
  • Sensorial: visual, auditory, tactile, gustatory, olfactory discrimination
  • Language: phonics, reading, writing, grammar, storytelling
  • Mathematics: counting, number operations, geometry, patterns
  • Cultural Studies: geography, science, botany, zoology, history
  • Creative Arts: music, painting, sculpture, dramatic play
Kindergarten Classroom
Ages 5-6

Kindergarten Program

Our Kindergarten Program provides the culminating Montessori experience, preparing children for a seamless transition to elementary school. Children at this level take on leadership roles in the classroom while advancing their academic skills.

Schedule

Full-day: 8:30 AM - 3:00 PM
Extended: 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM

Class Size

Maximum 24 children with 3 teachers (8:1 ratio)

Tuition

Full-day: $1,400/month
Extended: $1,650/month

Advanced Curriculum

  • Advanced reading comprehension and creative writing
  • Math operations, fractions, and word problems
  • Science experiments and nature studies
  • World cultures, geography, and global awareness
  • Research projects and presentations
  • Leadership and mentorship opportunities
Enrichment Activities
All Ages

Enrichment & After-School

Enhance your child's experience with our enrichment programs and after-school care. These activities complement the Montessori curriculum and provide opportunities for deeper exploration in areas of special interest.

Music & Movement

Rhythm, singing, and introduction to instruments

Spanish Language

Fun, immersive foreign language exposure

Garden Club

Planting, growing, and nature connection

Yoga & Mindfulness

Age-appropriate yoga and breathing exercises

Enrichment programs are included with full-day enrollment. Available as add-ons for half-day students at $100/month per activity.
