Summer Camp

A relaxed, hands-on Montessori summer with plenty of time outside.

Summer at Daffodils
Summer Program

Summer Days, Montessori Style

Summer camp keeps the calm routines of the school year and adds more outdoor time, art, and weekly themes. Children keep building independence while they explore, create, and play.

Camp is open to children ages 2 to 6, including current students and new families.

Dates and rates for next summer will be posted here. Join the interest list below and we will email you as soon as registration opens.
A Summer of Discovery

Weekly Themes

Each week centers on a theme with stories, projects, and hands-on work. Sample themes:

Garden & Growing

Planting, watering, and watching seeds sprout.

Art Studio

Painting, collage, clay, and color mixing.

Little Scientists

Sink or float, magnets, and nature experiments.

Around the Globe

Maps, music, and food from different countries.

Bugs & Critters

Life cycles, bug hunts, and outdoor observation.

Music & Movement

Rhythm, songs, dance, and simple instruments.

Join the Interest List

Get an email when summer camp dates and registration are announced.

A Typical Camp Day

Morning work time

Montessori materials and theme projects.

Outdoor play

Water play, gardening, and games outside.

Lunch and rest

Family-style lunch, then quiet time.

Afternoon art and stories

Art, music, and read-alouds before pickup.

What to Bring

Sunscreen (applied at home), a sun hat, a labeled water bottle, a change of clothes, and closed-toe shoes that can get wet.
