A quick guide to how our days work. Enrolled families can download the full handbook and forms from the Family Portal.

Hours and calendar

The school is open Monday through Friday. Program schedules are listed on our Programs page, and holidays, closures, and events are on the Events calendar and in the Family Portal.

Drop-off and pickup

Please walk your child in and hand them to a teacher, and sign them in at drop-off. Children are released only to parents and to adults listed on your child's pickup list in the Family Portal. We ask for photo ID from anyone we do not recognize.

If someone new will pick up, add them in the portal or call the office before pickup time. Please call if you are running late.

Illness and health

Please keep your child home if they have a fever of 100.4°F or higher, vomiting, diarrhea, a rash that has not been checked by a doctor, or are too unwell to take part in the day. Children should be free of fever, vomiting, and diarrhea for 24 hours without medicine before returning.

If your child becomes ill at school, we will call you to pick them up. Please report absences through the Family Portal so teachers know who to expect.

Medication and allergies

Staff can give medication only with a signed authorization form, in the original labeled container. Keep your child's allergies and medical notes current in the portal, and upload an allergy action plan if your child has one.

Immunization records

Washington requires a completed Certificate of Immunization Status (CIS) for every child in licensed child care. Please upload it in the Family Portal before your child's first day and whenever it is updated.

Meals and what to bring

Snacks and lunch are served daily; see the weekly Lunch Menu. Please send a labeled water bottle, a full change of clothes, and indoor shoes. Toddlers also need diapers or pull-ups and wipes. Please leave toys at home.

Staying in touch

Teachers post daily reports in the Family Portal, and you can message the office there at any time. Parent-teacher conferences are offered during the year; you can book a time in the portal when sign-ups open.

Tuition and withdrawal

Tuition is billed monthly; current rates are on the Tuition page. You can pay online in the Family Portal or by check. Please give the office written notice before withdrawing your child; your enrollment agreement explains the details.

Weather and emergency closures

If we need to close or open late for weather or an emergency, we will post it on the homepage banner and in the Family Portal and contact families directly.

Photos and privacy

We only share photos of children whose families have signed a photo release. Families-only photos are visible to enrolled families after logging in. Read our full Privacy Policy.

Nondiscrimination

Daffodils Montessori welcomes children and families of every race, color, religion, national origin, ancestry, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, and disability. We do not discriminate in admissions, programs, or employment, and we make reasonable accommodations for children with disabilities.
