About Our School

A Place Where Little Minds Grow Big Dreams

At Daffodils Montessori, we believe every child is a unique flower waiting to bloom. Our Montessori-inspired curriculum encourages independence, creativity, and a lifelong love of learning.

Founded with a passion for early childhood education, our school provides a nurturing environment where children ages 2-6 explore, discover, and grow at their own natural pace.
