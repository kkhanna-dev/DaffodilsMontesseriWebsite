Frequently Asked Questions

We accept children from ages 2 through 6. Our Toddler Program is for ages 2-3, our Primary Program is for ages 3-5, and our Kindergarten Program is for ages 5-6.

Tuition varies by program and schedule. Half-day rates start at $850/month and full-day rates start at $1,200/month. Please visit our Programs page or contact us for detailed pricing information.

Yes! We offer personalized campus tours during school hours. Tours typically last 45 minutes to an hour and include observation of classrooms in action. Contact us to schedule your visit.

Yes, we provide freshly prepared, nutritious lunches and snacks daily. Our kitchen accommodates common allergies and dietary restrictions. Parents may also choose to send lunch from home.

Our Toddler Program maintains a 4:1 ratio (12 children with 3 teachers), and our Primary and Kindergarten programs maintain an 8:1 ratio (24 children with 3 teachers).

The enrollment process includes: 1) Schedule a tour, 2) Submit an application, 3) Family interview, 4) Acceptance notification, 5) Complete enrollment paperwork and deposit. Visit our Enrollment page for details.
