## How Parents Register and Enroll

Here is the step-by-step process a parent goes through:

1. A parent visits your website and clicks **"Enroll Now"** or **"Login"**.
2. They create an account using their email and a password.
3. Once logged in, they go to their **Parent Portal** and add their child's information.
4. They fill out the **Contact form** to let you know they are interested.
5. You (the admin) review the information and **approve the child** in your dashboard.
6. The parent then selects a program and schedule on their portal.
7. The child is enrolled! That's it!

## Your Admin Dashboard - What You Can Do

When you log in, you have access to an admin dashboard with several tabs. Here is what each one does:

- **Interested Leads**: These are people who reached out through the website. This is a great list for follow-up phone calls!

- **Contact Submissions**: These are inquiries from parents who are logged in. You can mark each one as reviewed or contacted so you can keep track.

- **Enrollments**: These are applications that came in from the enrollment page. You can change the status of each one (for example, mark it as accepted or pending).

- **Tour Requests**: These are people who want to come visit the school. Give them a call to confirm a date and time!

- **Children Profiles**: See all the children in the system. When a parent adds their child, you will see them here. Click **"Approve"** to let the parent pick a program for their child.

- **Parent Accounts**: A list of all registered parents. Click on a name to see their full profile and details.

- **Curriculum Management** (NEW): Upload PDF documents for each program. Once uploaded, parents can see these documents on their portal. Great for sharing lesson plans, schedules, or handbooks.

- **Site Editor** (NEW): Change the text on your website without needing any technical help. You can update the hero text on the homepage, the about section, and more.

- **Announcements** (NEW): Post news and updates that show up on every parent's portal. Perfect for reminders about events, closures, or anything else parents need to know.
